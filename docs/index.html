How the score works

Each test scores 0–1 by how far a stock clears its threshold (for example ROIC earns 0 at a third of the bar and full marks at 1.67× it). The composite is the average × 100. Ranking is by tests passed first, so a stock passing all five always sits above one passing four.

Where the numbers come from

Fundamentals from Yahoo Finance, rescanned every weekday morning; prices and 52-week ranges refresh every 30 minutes while the market is open. ROIC and D/E are computed from the latest annual statements; PEG uses Yahoo's 5-year growth estimate, or P/E ÷ earnings growth when that is missing (marked "calc").

Email alerts

An automated job checks the buy-zone list every 30 minutes during market hours and emails you once per stock per day when one is within 5% of its 52-week low.

Before you buy

A screen finds candidates, not answers. Very low PEG, very high FCF yield or ROIC are flagged "check data": they often come from one-off gains or a shrinking business. This is a research tool, not personalized investment advice.
